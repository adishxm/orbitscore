## In simple words

Added an offline, safe evidence upload and extraction fallback to the FastAPI backend. Space operators and safety reviewers can upload synthetic mission datasheets (TXT or PDF), compute cryptographic SHA-256 integrity hashes, extract candidate telemetry fields with page/line source quotes, and mark every candidate unconfirmed (`human_confirmed=false`) by default until explicitly human-reviewed.

## What the team learned

By pairing deterministic regex extraction with a strict `human_confirmed=false` invariant, OrbitScore prevents unverified AI/regex inferences from creating accidental compliance certifications. Source quotes and exact line numbers enable reviewers to verify every telemetry claim against original documentation in seconds.

## Judge questions this unlocks

- What problem does this solve? It automates tedious telemetry extraction from mission datasheets while enforcing human-in-the-loop review, preventing unverified or hallucinated values from entering regulatory filings.
